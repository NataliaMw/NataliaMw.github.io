CASEFILE

Citation-verified evaluation of investigative reasoning in long-horizon agents

An agent gets a 300-document forensic-accounting archive. It has to rebuild what happened, cite evidence for every claim, catch the contradictions experts planted, and say what the record cannot answer. Every run gets a freshly generated archive.

  • 12 weeks · 30+ h/week
  • 1 domain committed: forensic accounting
  • ~350 expert hours
  • Natalia Mawyin

The gap

Agent benchmarks score the answer. Investigations fail on the evidence.

Forensic accounting engagements, internal investigations and due-diligence reviews are billable, high-value work. The core skill is building a defensible account from scattered, conflicting documents. Current agent benchmarks mostly grade a final answer, an end state or a whole deliverable. None of them checks, claim by claim, whether the evidence supports what the agent said.

FAILURE 1A fluent timeline on a citation that doesn't say thatThe quote exists. It just doesn't support the claim.
FAILURE 2A contradiction missed in a routine documentThe proof sits in a calendar entry, not in the email everyone reads.
FAILURE 3A confident answer the record can't supportThe right answer was "insufficient evidence", plus a next step.

One task, worked end to end

FA-07, "The Helpful Vendor"

Hotline tip: the AP manager is "taking care of" a new vendor312 items · email, chat, AP/GL ledger, vendor master log, receiving log, calendars · 1–4 h agent budget

Hidden ground truth (regenerated per seed)

  • Kestrel Industrial Supply was added under the AP manager's credentials; its bank account changed two weeks later.
  • Its address matches a relative's address in an HR form.
  • 14 invoices, $108,750. Nine, $81,450, have no goods receipt: six split just under the $10,000 dual-approval threshold, one of $14,200, two small ones. Totals are checked in code.

What experts planted

Contradiction C1. The $14,200 invoice carries the CFO's approval stamp, but the CFO's calendar shows a flight with no connectivity that day, and the audit log shows the approval came from the AP manager's workstation.

Unanswerable Q4. "Did the AP manager get paid by Kestrel?" There are no personal bank records. Correct answer: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, plus a next step.

The trap: a fluent memo that names the CFO as co-approver.

What it measures

Two headline numbers and a cost axis, not a weighted composite

Verified Event RecallExpert-weighted share of true events recovered. An event counts only if its citations exist and support it. Right answer, wrong evidence: zero.
Unsupported Assertion RateFabricated or non-supporting citations, plus confident answers to unanswerable questions, per 100 claims.
Cost per verified claimDollars and tool calls, on a score-vs-cost frontier next to a priced human expert baseline.

Diagnostics, reported separately

  • Contradiction detection precision and recall against planted pairs
  • Abstention calibration (Brier score)
  • Flag-then-assert gap: the agent flags a contradiction, then asserts the contradicted claim anyway
  • Isomorphic pass^k (all seeds of a template) vs. rerun pass^k, separating brittleness from noise
  • Expected error cost, with experts pricing each error type, and whether rankings survive their price ranges

Grader validity and contamination

The grader is validated, and contamination resistance is measured, not claimed

Grader agreement study

  • At least 60% of points come from deterministic or evidence-bound checks
  • Two forensic accountants double-label ~1,000 claims; Krippendorff's α per component, target ≥ 0.75
  • Judges from two or more model families vs. expert consensus, focused on false SUPPORTS
  • A component is automated only if judge-expert agreement is no worse than expert-expert agreement
  • Self-preference test and 30 planted near-miss submissions to attack the judge

Solver-bound instances

  • Every run draws fresh seeds; names, amounts, dates and which document carries the proof all move
  • Fine-tune an open model on gold answers for half the seeds, then measure gains on seen vs. unseen vs. frozen tasks
  • Answer-transplant test: paste another seed's gold timeline into the prompt
  • Archives generated by a model family not under test
